Our story

From Cameroon's Kitchen to Your Table

At Manyi's Tasty, every dish tells a story. We bring the rich culinary traditions of Cameroon and West Africa to the heart of Essen — with recipes passed down through generations, prepared with fresh ingredients and genuine love.

Whether it's our legendary roasted fish, the comforting warmth of a perfect Achu, or the deep flavours of Ndolé — each plate is a celebration of heritage, community, and the art of gathering around food.
